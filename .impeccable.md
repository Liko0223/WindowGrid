## Design Context

### Users
WindowGrid is for macOS users who keep several windows open at once: developers, designers, operators, researchers, and anyone using wide or multi-display workspaces. They want to arrange windows quickly without learning a heavy tiling window manager or leaving the native macOS feel.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and native. The product should feel like a small professional utility that quietly gives the desktop more structure.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined macOS-inspired visual language: crisp panels, clean rhythm, soft material depth, and a restrained palette pulled from the app icon's coral, sky, amber, and violet quadrants. Avoid generic SaaS hero sections, heavy dark neon treatment, or marketing-heavy copy.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the product job: drag a window, see the grid, release into place.
- Keep the site lightweight, direct, and trustworthy.
- Make download and setup requirements immediately clear.
- Let the app icon and grid behavior carry the visual identity.
- Favor native-feeling polish over decorative complexity.
